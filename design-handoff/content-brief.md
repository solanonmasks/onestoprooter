## 6. Service pages

### Drain Cleaning
**Headline:** Serving the Lower Mainland Area With Drain Cleaning Services

- Residential and commercial drain cleaning
- Clearing clogs is one of their most common jobs
- Message: even a partial clog can damage pipes and cause buildup, so deal with slow drains early

**Warning signs to call:** puddles near fixtures, foul odours from the drain, gurgling noises, pooling water

**Three sub-services:**
- **Kitchen Drain Cleaning:** clogs from grease, soap, fats, and detergents. Don't DIY; they have current equipment and skills.
- **Bathroom Sink Drain Cleaning:** clogs from soap scum, hair, toothpaste, beauty products. Camera inspection used to find the problem fast.
- **Bathtub Drain Cleaning:** clogged tubs lead to bigger issues later; cleaning done to prevent backups and costly repairs. Mentions estimates and senior discounts.

**Banner:** "Day or Night, We Fix it Right" with a link to Emergency Plumbing

### Sewer Lines / Camera Inspection
**Headline:** Camera Inspection and Sewer Line Repairs in the Lower Mainland and Surrounding Areas

- Full-service, 24-hour company offering sewer line repair

**Problems they fix:**
- Broken pipes
- Sewer blockages
- Leaking joints
- Tree roots
- Corrosion
- Off-grade pipes
- Bellied pipes

- Also does visual inspections of sewer lines and other underground pipes

**Subsection: Camera Inspection**

**Banner:** "We Do Installation and Repair Work" (kitchen drain repairs to garburator installs)

### Broken Water Lines
**Headline:** Water Line Repair and Service in the Lower Mainland

- Damaged lines cause wet lawn areas, low pressure, and high bills
- Causes: mineral buildup, seismic events, freeze and thaw, high water pressure, general wear
- Early warning signs: reduced pressure, discoloured water
- Certified plumbers; licensed, bonded, and insured; available any time, all week

**Signs of a Damaged Water Line:**
- Parts of the yard stay damp and mushy
- Water bill consistently higher than normal
- Water meter moves even with the inside shutoff valve closed

**Subsection: Well-Trained Professionals for Water Line Repair**
- In-depth assessment before any repair
- Video camera system pinpoints cause and location
- Underground repair with minimal impact on the lawn
- Open, honest communication

**Banner:** "No Dig Water Main Replacement" (new pipe installed inside the old one, no lawn excavation)

### Installation and Repair
**Headline:** Pipe Installation and Repair Services in the Lower Mainland

- Covers everything from emergency drain and burst pipe repairs to garburator installs and pipe replacements

**Sub-services:**
- **Perimeter Drainage Repair and Installation:** keeps excess water away from the house; failures lead to damp crawl spaces or basements and structural risk
- **Installation of Water Lines and Sewer Lines:** for new home builds. Process: digging, setting slope, preparing the trench bed, laying pipe, connecting to the home
- **Kitchen Drain Repairs:** for recurring clogs and odours; quick, cost-effective fixes
- **Bathtub Drain Repairs:** stopper assembly, leaks, and clogs
- **Shower Drain Repairs:** diagnose, discuss options, give an estimate, finish on time
- **Toilet Repairs:** residential and commercial; unclogging, leaks, installs, replacements; 24/7
- **Sink Repairs:** clogs, leaks, repairs, and installs for kitchens and bathrooms
- **Faucet Repairs:** drips and leaks that raise water bills
- **Leak Repairs:** sewer pipes, bathrooms, kitchens, basements, and more
- **Garburator Repair and Installation:** unit recommendations, install, and proper connection to power and plumbing

### Sewer Pump & Sump Pump
**Headline:** Reliable Sewer Pump Installation in the Lower Mainland Area

- Flooded basement often points to a faulty sump pump
- Sump pumps reduce flooding and route water away from the foundation's lowest point
- Licensed, insured, experienced pros for installs and repairs
- They assess whether a new install or a repair is needed
- Trained in local and municipal codes so systems pass inspection

**Types of sump pumps:**
- **Pedestal:** mounted above the pit, easy access for service and testing
- **Submersible:** installed inside the pit, out of sight

- Also installs battery backups; recommends a water level alarm

**Banner:** "We Are Ready to Roll Up Our Sleeves"

### Garburator (Install and Repair)
**Headline:** Garburator Installation and Repair Services in the Lower Mainland Area

- Fixes slow, smelly, and clogged garburators quickly
- Short explainer of what a garburator does and where it sits (under the sink, between drain and trap)

**Taking Care of the Disposal Unit: foods to keep out**
- Grease
- Fibrous foods
- Starchy food
- Coffee grounds
- Fruit pits, seeds, and apple cores
- Eggshells
- Bones

**Signs of a faulty garburator:**
- Unusual noises, or no noise at all
- Slow drains where the unit is installed
- Unpleasant odours
- Foreign objects stuck in the blades
- Overflowing or leaks in the sink or pipes

**Subsection: Upgrade or Repair?**
- Units over 5 years old should be checked regularly
- Upgrading is often the better call: more energy efficient, quieter, fewer repair bills
- They help the customer decide

**Banner:** "Working Garburator in No Time"
